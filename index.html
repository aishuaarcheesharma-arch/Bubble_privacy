BubbleWorld Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

BubbleWorld is an educational and entertainment game intended primarily for children aged 7–12. This policy describes information that may be processed when you use the app.

1. Developer and contact

Developer: Aarchee Sharma
Email: aishuaarcheesharma@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your device

BubbleWorld is designed to store game progress and preferences locally on your device where supported. This may include completed levels, scores, stars, achievements, language and settings preferences, and artwork created in the app. We do not intentionally send this locally stored game information to our own servers.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored information. Device backups or operating-system features may behave differently.

3. Personal information

The game does not require an account to play and does not intentionally ask children to provide their names, phone numbers, home addresses, or email addresses through game forms.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

BubbleWorld may display banner or interstitial advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising services may process information to deliver, measure, protect, and troubleshoot ads. Depending on the SDK, configuration, and device settings, this may include device and app information, IP/network information, advertising or ad-interaction information, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information.

The app is intended for children. Advertising SDKs and settings must be configured to comply with applicable Google Play Families requirements and child-privacy laws. We aim to use appropriate child-directed treatment and age-appropriate ad content where required, but the exact data processed depends on the SDK version and configuration in the published app.

Read Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

5. Internet access and permissions

An internet connection may be used to load ads and open online pages such as this policy. Core gameplay may work offline where supported. Permissions and behavior can vary by app version.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell children's personal information. We do not intentionally share locally stored game progress or artwork with our own servers. Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process information as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect app security and integrity.

7. Retention and deletion

Game data stored locally remains on the device until deleted through an available in-app control, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling the app, subject to device backup behavior. Data processed independently by third-party providers is subject to their own retention and deletion policies; we cannot directly delete information held by those providers.

8. Children's privacy and parental contact

We take children's privacy seriously and aim to comply with applicable child-privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions or concerns about the app's privacy practices. We will review requests and respond as appropriate under applicable law.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and its data. However, no app, device, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-party services and links

The app may open external websites, including this policy and Google's privacy resources. Those websites operate under their own policies. We are not responsible for websites we do not operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same URL, and the effective date above will be revised when appropriate.
